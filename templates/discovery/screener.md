# Discovery screener (signup form)

Three questions, in this order, before anything is scheduled. The answers go
in the participant row's `screener` block and are the first data point.

1. **Are you actively job searching, or do you coach people who are?**
   (job searching / career change / coach / recruiter or hiring / school or
   workforce program / none of these)
2. **What do you use today to keep track of applications, résumés, and
   interviews?** (free text; capture tool names verbatim)
3. **Which AI assistant do you use most, if any?** (ChatGPT / Claude /
   Copilot / Gemini / other / none)

Then:

- Name, email, and the best 30-minute window this week or next.
- One line: "I'm doing customer discovery for jobContext. This is a
  conversation, not a demo. $25 gift card for 30 minutes, or a year of
  jobContext Pro if you'd rather. You can stop at any point."

Screen out: "none of these" on question 1, unless they coach or hire.
Screen in first: anyone who names a tool on question 2 (they have a workflow
to compare against).
