# Participant consent (version 2026-09)

**What this is.** A 30-minute conversation (or, for beta testers, up to three
sessions) about how you manage your job search, run by Frank MacBride,
founder of jobContext LLC, to improve the product.

**What I collect.** Your name and email, your answers to three signup
questions, notes from our conversation, and, if you agree below, an audio
recording. Beta testers may also choose to load their own résumé and career
history into a jobContext workspace; that data is theirs, lives in their own
partition, and is deleted on request.

**How it's used.** To decide what to build. Aggregates (counts, themes) may
appear in public materials and applications. Nothing that identifies you is
published. A quote may be used only if you tick the box below, and then only
as "a job seeker" / "a career coach", never by name or employer.

**Your control.** You can stop at any time, skip any question, and ask me to
delete your notes, recording, or workspace by emailing
frankvmacbride@gmail.com. Deletion is done within 7 days and confirmed.

**Incentive.** $25 gift card per completed interview, $50 for completing
three beta sessions, or a 12-month jobContext Pro grant instead. The
incentive is yours regardless of what you say.
