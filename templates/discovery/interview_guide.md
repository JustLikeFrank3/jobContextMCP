# Interview guide (30 minutes)

Rules: ask about the past, not the future. No demo unless they ask. Write
down the words they use for things. If they say "I just keep it in my head",
that is a finding.

**0–3 min. Context.** Confirm consent and recording. "Tell me about the last
time you applied for something you actually wanted."

**3–12 min. The workflow as it exists.** Walk one real application end to
end. Where did the job description come from? Where does the résumé live?
How many versions? What happened after you submitted? What did you have to
re-explain, and to whom (a person, a portal, an AI)?

**12–20 min. AI.** "The last time you used an AI assistant for anything
career-related, what did you ask it, and what did you do with the answer?"
Then: did it ever say something about you that wasn't true? How did you
notice? What did you do?

**20–26 min. Pain ranking.** "Of everything you've described, what's the one
part you'd pay to make go away?" Then: what have you already tried or paid
for? Why did you stop?

**26–30 min. Close.** "Who else should I talk to?" Confirm the incentive.
Do not pitch. If they ask what jobContext is, answer in one sentence and
offer a follow-up.

Afterwards, within the hour: session notes from the template, real duration,
themes, and the incentive sent with its reference in the ledger.
