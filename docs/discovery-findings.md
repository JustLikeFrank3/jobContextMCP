## How to read this

Interview counts follow the rules in docs/discovery.md: completed, at least
20 minutes, consent on file, non-internal participant. Themes are tags
assigned after each call and counted across interviews and completed beta
sessions. Quotes appear only with the participant's explicit quote consent
and are attributed to a segment, never a person.
