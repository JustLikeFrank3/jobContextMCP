HARBORLINE TECHNOLOGIES
Senior Backend Engineer, Logistics Platform
Remote (US) | Full-time

ABOUT HARBORLINE

Harborline Technologies builds the freight-visibility platform used by
shippers and 3PLs to track containerized cargo across ocean, rail, and
road. Our event pipeline processes 4 billion tracking events per year
from carrier EDI feeds, IoT sensors, and port APIs, and we hold
ourselves to a 99.99% availability target for the tracking API our
customers embed in their own products.

THE ROLE

We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer for the Logistics Platform
team. You will own services in the ingestion and normalization tier:
the systems that take raw carrier events in a dozen formats and turn
them into the clean, deduplicated timeline our customers see.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

- Design and operate high-throughput event-processing services in
  Java and Spring Boot on Kubernetes.
- Evolve our Kafka topology as event volume grows, including
  partitioning strategy and consumer-group design.
- Lead incremental decomposition of remaining monolith components
  into independently deployable services.
- Set the bar for testing and observability on the team: coverage
  gates in CI, SLO-based alerting, actionable runbooks.
- Mentor mid-level engineers through design review and pairing.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

- 7+ years building backend services in Java; Spring Boot in
  production at scale.
- Hands-on Kafka experience: partitioning, consumer rebalancing,
  exactly-once semantics and their limits.
- A track record of monolith-to-services migrations that shipped
  without customer-facing downtime.
- Cost consciousness: you have measurably reduced infrastructure
  spend without trading away reliability.
- Experience holding a contractual SLA and the operational habits
  that come with it: on-call, incident command, blameless reviews.
- PostgreSQL depth: query planning, partitioning, connection
  pooling under load.
- Kubernetes in production; Terraform or equivalent IaC.

NICE TO HAVE

- Experience with EDI or other legacy B2B integration formats.
- Prior work in freight, logistics, or supply-chain software.
- Open-source contributions or self-hosted side projects.

Harborline is an equal opportunity employer. Compensation range for
this role is $165,000 - $205,000 base plus equity.
